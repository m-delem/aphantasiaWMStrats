# WM-FTT: structure among the questionnaire scales

**Status: stub, created 2026-08-24 during the restructure.** Exploratory
strand, and the first step of the unplanned-groups arc that
`13-clustering.md` continues.

## The question

The study administers several instruments (VVIQ, OSIVQ, NIEQ, TAS-20) that
plausibly measure overlapping constructs. Before clustering participants,
it is worth knowing how the scales themselves relate: which are
redundant, which carry independent information, and whether the imagery
measures separate from the alexithymia and inner-experience ones.

## What belongs here

- Partial correlations among scale scores, which is the minimum honest
  version of "how do these relate", since raw correlations among
  overlapping instruments are hard to read.
- Dimension reduction, if the partial correlation structure justifies it.
- A statement of which scales enter `13-clustering.md`'s feature set and
  why, so that decision is made here rather than inside the clustering
  doc.

## Sequencing

Depends on `07-questionnaire-psychometrics.md`: a scale that fails its own
reliability check should not enter a correlation structure as though it
were measured. Feeds `13-clustering.md`.

## Standing constraint

This is exploratory and must be labelled as such throughout. The
confirmatory strand (`08` through `11`) uses VVIQ-defined groups fixed in
advance. Anything found here is hypothesis-generating.
