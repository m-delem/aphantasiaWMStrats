# WM-FTT: questionnaire psychometrics

**See the vignette.** `vignettes/articles/psychometrics.Rmd` covers VVIQ,
OSIVQ and NIEQ as they behave in this sample.

Distinct from `06-task-validity.md`, which asks whether the *task*
measures anything stable; this asks whether the *instruments* do. Both
have to hold before an association between them means anything.

Findings that constrain downstream work, recorded here so a modelling
session does not have to open the vignette to find them:

- The VVIQ/OSIVQ multitrait-multimethod pattern test **fails**, and this
  note needs replacing with what it actually failed on. It is not a
  convergence failure: VVIQ correlates 0.86 with OSIVQ-object against 0.15
  to 0.38 with spatial and verbal. As written the note reads as a caution
  against merging them, which `12` §3 argues is exactly what should be
  done.
- NIEQ sensory-focus pair consistency is **0.49**, below threshold. That
  dimension was already the weakest in the published validation.
  Unsymbolised thinking is the dimension of substantive interest.
- **OSIVQ reverse-keying is inconsistent across versions**: v1 and v2 hold
  unreversed values in the raw item columns, v3 holds reversed ones.
